The Pultec Trick
The EQ that boosts and cuts at the same time
The Pultec EQP-1A is one of the most loved equalizers ever made. But if you've only used modern parametric EQs, it behaves… strangely. In this short interactive module you'll play with a virtual Pultec, see its curves, and learn the famous “Pultec trick”: boosting and cutting the same frequencies at once.
Try it: Want to hear it too? Use the ▶ button to play a short looping clip through the EQ — then, on each unit, move the knobs to hear the curve, not just see it. It keeps playing as you move through the module, so you can listen right along. It's completely optional, though: you can go through the whole module with the sound off and not miss a thing.
